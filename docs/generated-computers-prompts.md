# Generated Apple-style computer and tablet illustrations

Built-in ImageGen was used without supplying Apple photographs as reference inputs. Each listed finish and product was generated as a separate image with a genuine transparent background. The generated pictures are illustrative rather than guaranteed accurate depictions of physical devices. Final assets are 940-pixel-wide alpha-preserving WebP images in `client/public/apple-store/generated/`; the mapping of product id and color to image is `scripts/generated-computers.json`.

## Prompt templates

- Tablets (seven individual runs): “Premium photorealistic product illustration for an electronics catalog; one Apple [model] in [specified finish], rear three-quarter view, thin aluminum body with appropriate cameras, complete isolated device, breathable margins, realistic proportions and materials, soft studio lighting; true transparent alpha; no props, floor, shadows, text or watermark.” Models: iPad Pro 13 and 11 (black); iPad Air 13 and 11 (sky blue); iPad A16 (light blue); iPad mini (midnight and starlight).
- Laptops (eleven individual runs): “Premium photorealistic product illustration for a mobile electronics catalog; one Apple [model] in [specified finish], laptop open in front three-quarter view, slim metal body and black keyboard, abstract screen wallpaper, complete object visible with margins, specified case color clear, true transparent alpha, soft studio lighting; no scene, props, floor, shadows, text or watermark.” Models and finishes: MacBook Neo blush, silver, citrus, indigo; MacBook Air sky blue, silver, starlight, midnight; MacBook Pro standard black; MacBook Pro PROLED black and silver.
- Desktops, displays and headset (six individual runs): “Premium photorealistic product illustration for a mobile electronics catalog; exactly one [model], refined three-quarter view, realistic materials and finish, soft abstract screen wallpaper where relevant, studio lighting and clean edges, true transparent alpha, no people, unrelated accessories, floor, shadows, text, watermark or duplicate objects.” Models: iMac, Mac mini, Mac Studio, Studio Display, Studio Display XDR, Vision Pro.

All 24 files were checked for an alpha channel with fully transparent pixels and inspected on contact sheets. Each generated image may differ in small details or exact proportions from the real product.
