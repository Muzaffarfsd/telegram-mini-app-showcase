# China buyer — v2 product image notes

Ten single-subject editorial product ideas were independently generated with the built-in imagegen tool, visually reviewed as a contact sheet and compressed to WebP at 900 px wide. Their purpose is a more diverse demonstration assortment; none are verified products, brand stock, known prices or material specifications. The series uses light stone, graphite and intentional wine-red touches to connect to the new China-inspired art direction without implying any specific supplier.
