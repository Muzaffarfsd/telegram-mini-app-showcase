# China buyer v2 — creative direction

User feedback F-20261104-034 rejected the first homepage as repetitive and the product detail as too shallow. The new opening uses a large original brush-ink dragon and restrained red editorial accents to give this specific buyer demo a character distinct from LUMI and the fashion store. The opening copy speaks to buyers: a personal collection where clients can explore and prepare a request. Below it, a curated showcase, expressive product still-life, buyer story and a concrete customer journey demonstrate the concept. Generated people, goods and settings are fictional; the interface continues to make the demo limitations clear.

The catalogue grows from 8 to 18 ideas with ten newly generated product images. Four highlighted ideas have a main image and three supplementary AI-generated images, reviewed for overall color, silhouette and major details. Minor generated details may vary and are labelled accordingly in the interface. Other ideas show labelled crops of their single image, not other products pretending to be views of the same item.

Visual QA: opening at 320/390/430px; four-image gallery and buyer section at 390px. Functional QA: Chromium and WebKit across the three widths, full catalogue and request workflow; see the two buyer E2E scripts. Buyer-request data remains a local demonstration and is not submitted to a real operator.
