# China buyer — second iteration hero art

User rejected the initial buyer-app visual direction as too generic across the demos and requested a distinctive China-inspired lead, suggesting a large bold hand-painted dragon or temple. This iteration generates an original large black calligraphic dragon for the hero, supported by new editorial photographs: one shows a fictional buyer handling a garment in a Guangzhou-inspired atelier, and the other depicts an imaginative handbag-and-fabric still life with a city backdrop. The intended effect is expressive, modern and visibly more distinctive while remaining legible on a light mobile interface.

All three images were created with the built-in imagegen tool, visually reviewed as stills, optimized with sharp to 1000px wide WebP, and contain no in-image text. The generated dragon's real transparency was checked against a white background and preserved; recommended use is a large object-contained image with ample contrast and interface copy placed separately. Generated people, goods and locations are editorial concepts, not real staff, stock, suppliers or verified locations.

- `client/public/china-buyer/v2-dragon-ink.webp` — four-channel transparent S-shaped hand-painted dragon with subtle vermilion brush accent; 1000×1500, 471982 bytes.
- `client/public/china-buyer/v2-buyer-work.webp` — portrait-oriented fashion sourcing scene in oxblood/black/stone; 1000×1500, 190268 bytes.
- `client/public/china-buyer/v2-objects-story.webp` — sculptural handbag, fabric and ribbon with a Guangzhou-inspired skyline; 1000×1500, 153502 bytes.

Visual review in the final mobile hero and in all breakpoints remains necessary; the user has not yet evaluated this iteration.
