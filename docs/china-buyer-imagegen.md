# China buyer — editorial image direction

Six independently generated still photographs support a calm, premium buyer service for clients. The creative direction combines visual depth and expressive photography with a light, readable interface, drawing on the user's prior feedback F-20261104-021 and F-20261104-029 without presenting this unreviewed design as approved. A single ink-black, white and grey palette with restrained wine accents gives the app a coherent editorial rhythm.

All six are fictional AI-generated editorial illustrations, not real buyer portraits, shops, factories, products or evidence of service delivery. Asset descriptions, catalog cards and labels must make this distinction clear. They were visually inspected as stills and optimized to approximately 1000 px wide WebP for mobile; crops require review in the rendered interface.

- `/china-buyer/hero.webp`: portrait of a fictional fashion buyer in a bright showroom with a Guangzhou-like skyline.
- `/china-buyer/fashion.webp`: model in a contemporary tailored outfit on a city street, for mood and styling.
- `/china-buyer/bags.webp`: sculptural burgundy handbag, fictional category inspiration.
- `/china-buyer/shoes.webp`: black loafers arranged on a stone ledge, fictional category inspiration.
- `/china-buyer/sourcing.webp`: hands evaluating the fabric and seam of a tailored garment; editorial illustration of attention to details, not an assurance of real-world inspection.
- `/china-buyer/city.webp`: generated modern architecture and a Guangzhou-inspired city view, atmospheric imagery rather than a photograph of a verified address.

Integration review: the process photo, editorial heading, FAQ and request button were checked in the actual detail screen at 320 and 390 px in Chromium and WebKit. All four FAQ sections opened, the CTA led to the request form, image decoded, horizontal overflow was absent, and no page errors were observed. The 320 px screenshot was visually reviewed; text and imagery remained readable.
