# Nike sneaker demo — editorial images

Four images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool for the November 2026 redesign. They are editorial mood images of fictional unbranded sneakers, not exact photos of any catalog model. Keep the existing user-requested posters. Keep interface copy outside these images so it stays readable.

The direction applies F-20261102-005 (light interface, expressive shoe photography) and the local composition lesson F-20261103-002 (a playful change in scale). The scale study is an addition, not a replacement for the two separately requested poster treatments. F-20261103-001 informs the compact top spacing when integrating the images. The images were visually inspected as stills; the interface crop and rendering still need verification at mobile widths.

All files are in `client/public/royal-step/`, resized to 1000px wide and encoded as WebP:

- `editorial-motion-v2.webp`: daylight sports-fashion photograph of a woman on pale concrete stairs, sneaker prominent in the foreground. Prompt: premium mobile editorial, low camera, white and silver fictional sneaker, believable anatomy, real materials, no typography, logo or watermark.
- `editorial-detail-v2.webp`: close photograph of white leather, grey mesh, stitches and sculpted sole. Prompt: architectural macro, soft studio light, rich natural textures, no branding or text.
- `editorial-city-v2.webp`: two adults seated on pale urban steps in neutral streetwear, with white and charcoal fictional sneakers. Prompt: street-style lookbook, shoe-level framing, daylight, natural proportions, no branding or text.
- `editorial-scale-v2.webp`: giant fictional white and silver sneaker and a small adult figure in a light architectural studio. Prompt: striking campaign study of scale, realistic materials, directional sunlight and no type, branding or watermark.

The images have not been verified against Nike product specifications or exact catalog colorways. Use them for atmosphere and collections, not as a named product view or evidence of its construction.

## Integration review

The intended effect is a clean, tactile sneaker editorial: a larger Jordan feature opens the home, followed by the two preserved posters, product discovery, and varied human, macro and scale studies. Copy stays separate from images; category links lead to the existing demo catalogue. The product page uses one primary view and three alternatives, so the shoe remains the focus without sacrificing the path to size and purchase. F-20261104-030 authorizes this expansion; it does not imply that the user has approved the final result.

The integrated editorial crops and category links were reviewed on 320, 390 and 430 px screens in Chromium. The motion crop was adjusted to keep both the model and shoe visible. Existing 64 generated product views were reviewed as contact sheets: major color and silhouette were consistent; precise SKU details could not be verified.
