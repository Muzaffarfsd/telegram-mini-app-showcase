# Apple product presentation — image generation notes

Built-in ImageGen was used with the corresponding Apple product render as a visual reference for each model. The project-ready, alpha-preserving WebP assets are in `client/public/apple-store/creative/`; the source PNGs remain in the local ImageGen library.

Prompt-set summary: “Create a high-end photorealistic alternate product shot for a mobile product gallery. Preserve the specific model's silhouette, materials, finish, controls and camera geometry from the reference. Use a genuine transparent alpha background with clean antialiased edges; no scene, floor, cast shadow, border, text, watermark, or unrelated products. Use soft, precise studio lighting.” The subject and angle were specified separately for each device: rear or camera detail for phones and tablets; three-quarter product angles for watches, computers, displays, speakers, the headset, TV and accessories; a floating earbud-and-case arrangement for AirPods Pro. The two Hermès images use unlettered black screens to avoid invented numerals.

The generated frames are illustrations, and may differ in small physical details from the actual products. The primary product photos and additional verified Apple-hosted angles are kept distinct from generated frames. The gallery visibly labels generated images and links to official specifications. Apple-hosted images remain subject to a separate rights review.

Design direction: apply the user’s F-20261103-007 correction and the direct Apple references to a clear white product presentation, with a browsable image strip, concise product features and actual specifications. Check alpha edges, proportions and mobile behaviour on the real rendered page.
