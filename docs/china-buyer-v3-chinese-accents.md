# Chinese brush accents in the buyer demo

F-20261104-035 asks for meaningful Chinese calligraphy, red and/or cloud motifs in the existing buyer app. The visual treatment places a small `寻` (search) seal by the dragon, `寻找` (to search) in the catalogue, `精选` (carefully selected) at the editorial divider and `选` (select) as a subtle card mark. Russian copy remains readable and Chinese marks are decorative. `精选` is documented by the [Ministry of Education dictionary](https://dict.concised.moe.edu.tw/dictView.jsp?ID=23589&la=0&powerMode=0); `寻找` is documented in [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/chinese-simplified-english/%E5%AF%BB%E6%89%BE).

Characters are real text in a self-hosted subset of [Ma Shan Zheng](https://github.com/googlefonts/mashanzheng) by Ma ShanZheng, from Google Fonts; the adjacent `ma-shan-zheng-OFL.txt` preserves its SIL Open Font License. This avoids generated pseudo-characters and device-dependent glyph fallback. The red cloud motif was generated with the built-in imagegen tool as a transparent illustration; it is an expressive interpretation, not a historical reproduction. A ruyi-shaped cloud motif is described by the [Palace Museum](https://young.dpm.org.cn/en/article/1128).

The client home and catalogue, 320px product brief and 320/390/430px Chromium and WebKit renders were inspected. Decorative assets are noninteractive and do not carry essential meaning. The cloud was moved away from the catalogue introduction and the product brief title after visual review; CSS geometry replaces the former emoji star to keep its color consistent in WebKit.
