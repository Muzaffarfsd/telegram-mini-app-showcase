# Fixed living-room research asset

`fixed-living-room.glb` is a derivative of the [img2interior living-room showcase source](https://github.com/img2threejs/img2threejs-showcase/releases/tag/img2interior-living-room-assets-v1), authored by hoainho/img2threejs. The original static and native scene is made available by that release; the source's notices identify some materials and HDRIs from Poly Haven as CC0. The release does **not** state that the complete scene or its synthetic reference are CC0. Rights for commercial redistribution of this derivative have not been established.

The original movable sofa, chair, coffee table and their loose tabletop objects were removed from the packed Blender master before rebaking. Architecture, built-in decor and lighting are fixed. New combined-light atlases were baked in Cycles using 32 samples: architecture/furniture/textiles at 2048², garden/details at 1024². The artwork print retains its separate original image and UVs to preserve detail; its frame uses simple materials. Static meshes were merged by atlas, except for the independently hidden walls and ceiling. The atlas is baked without the products loaded by the app; dynamic products are separate GLBs from the catalogue and use approximate contact shading. This is a designed room, not a scan or measurement of a customer's home.

The source master, conversion script and comparison captures are in `work/img2interior-study/` and `work/casa-ocaso-study/` in the parent workspace. The scene is a work in progress and has only been exercised in desktop browser emulation, not on physical mobile GPUs.
